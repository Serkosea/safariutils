## Additions & Improvements

- Added shared-party hotspot assignments and objective updates across compatible mods, including held Shining Coins
- Improved Sparkling detection using additional skin and texture information, including before ticket use and across biomes
- Refined `Full Clear`, `Unique` and `Sparkling` mode visibility and biome-completion reporting
- Added self-lookups when `/safari lookup`, `/safari tickets` or `/safari missing` is used without a name
- Improved lookup caching, cooldown messages, Hunting levels and chat formatting

## Fixes

- Fixed mode-specific automatic ticket selection
- Fixed lingering caught Sparkling state and repeated discovery alerts, including late arrivals and reconnects
- Preserved hotspot assignments through connection renewal and prevented assignment prompts for unticketed guests
- Fixed final bird-feeder consumption leaving stale remaining items
- Improved ticket-trading warp timing around party joins and island transfers
- Matched `mini…` and `m…` lobby IDs for shared-party connections
