## Changes & Additions

- Added automatic network party sync to Normal and Extra builds for objectives and Sparkling discoveries
- Added persistent use-ticket reminders for shared Sparkling discoveries, including outside Ticket Trading
- Reorganized settings with clearer groups, search navigation, consistent controls, and smoother hover effects
- Combined normal and special themes in one picker, with independent special-theme colors and improved gradient effects
- Unified critter and recatch pity titles under one setting

## Fixes

- Fixed lost pity, stuck markers, and duplicate markers during rapid Hideon recatches and body changes
- Fixed temporary Duplico bodies leaving static markers and shrinking markers ignoring special critter colors
- Fixed delayed party-shared Sparkling loading and missing question marks for unsynced objective inventories
- Made Hideyho alerts follow marker visibility settings and added missing Hideyho and Shyworm chat filters
- Fixed settings hover consistency, inline text editing, and theme-popup focus behavior

## Downloads

Choose one jar for your Minecraft version:

- `safariutils-3.1.0+mc26.1.2.jar`
- `safariutils-3.1.0-extra+mc26.1.2.jar`
- `safariutils-3.1.0+mc26.2.jar`
- `safariutils-3.1.0-extra+mc26.2.jar`

The regular jars use Safe Mode; Extra includes toggles in the Safe Mode category that when turned off, provide information the player cannot directly see and may not be safe to use; Minecraft 26.1.2 builds have received limited testing compared to 26.2.
