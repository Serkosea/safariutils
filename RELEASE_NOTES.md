## Fixes

- Fixed automatic Sparkling ticket entry repeatedly reopening the pause menu while Minecraft is unfocused (`EXTRA ONLY`).
- Removed unnecessary ground-contact and travel-distance restrictions during automatic ticket entry, with a three-second approach timeout (`EXTRA ONLY`).
- Fixed leftover critter textures creating stuck markers after grouped catches, including Mantis Shrimp.
- Improved texture ownership through captures and breakouts, with clearer Sparkling appearance diagnostics.
