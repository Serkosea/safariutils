## Fixes & Improvements

- Improved Sparkling imports with a per-critter breakdown, including imports that add zero catches. Use `/safari importsparkling` with an instance directory to include archived logs from another installation.
- Fixed Rockmite catch capsules appearing as mound waypoints and reduced false Sparkling markers when critters are close together.
- Expanded the developer party-sync activity feed to show objective progress, biome reports and Sparkling discoveries and catches.

## Downloads

Choose one jar for your Minecraft version:

`safariutils-3.9.1+mc26.3.jar`
`safariutils-3.9.1+mc26.2.jar`
`safariutils-3.9.1+mc26.1.2.jar`
`safariutils-3.9.1-extra+mc26.3.jar`
`safariutils-3.9.1-extra+mc26.2.jar`
`safariutils-3.9.1-extra+mc26.1.2.jar`

Regular jars use Safe Mode; Extra includes Safe Mode toggles that when turned off, provide information the player cannot directly see and may not be safe to use. Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.3 builds have received less in-game testing than 26.2.
