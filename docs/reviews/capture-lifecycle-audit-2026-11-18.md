# Capture lifecycle audit

## Ownership contract

CritterEntities publishes one paired observation snapshot, including positive capsule scaffolding evidence. CritterState owns logical individuals, attempts, outcomes and body replacement. CaptureEffects projects its decisions into RecatchSpots, StillCritters and SparklingWatch. Consumers must not independently infer that an entity UUID change is a new discovery, or that a species-only catch identifies a particular neighbor.

## Corrections

- Active identity reassignment now notifies SparklingWatch through CaptureEffects, just as idle replacement does. Local presentation, queued chat and outstanding discovery aliases follow the individual.
- Rendering, recatch body learning and throw prediction use shared capture-scaffolding classification rather than separate incomplete capsule checks.
- Delayed breakout returns and capture-label ownership are tightened in CritterState with batch ambiguity checks and regression coverage.
- The earlier remote Hideonfloor fix filters capsule labels and retires only an unambiguous absent remembered origin. It does not award catches or publish completion.

## Boundaries

UUID/label/texture evidence is stronger than proximity. Simultaneous live peers must remain independent. Ambiguous evidence must not retire or steal a neighbor. Breakouts preserve pity and discovery presentation; actual catch resolution retires only the matched individual. Species-level party evidence cannot prove which of two local individuals was caught.

No new networking, disk polling or per-frame world scans. Existing scan publication and attempt projection remain in place. This is a correction of the central lifecycle, not a replacement with another parallel tracker.

## Remaining validation

In-game group testing is still necessary: another player catching a nearby ordinary critter, repeated group breakouts, a Sparkling breakout with changed body/label IDs, and late/out-of-range returns. Logs should show a single logical discovery and no capsule counted as a live critter. Automated checks cannot prove every server packet ordering.

Validation: 26.1.2 and isolated 26.3 checks passed; the earlier overlapping 26.3 invocation failed on a missing compiled class and passed after rebuilding alone. Regression coverage includes 332 recorded capture inputs, delayed/ambiguous returns, competing capsule labels, and a 50-block throw. Capture-label rejection diagnostics emit only on changed summaries. Long-range matching uses target-relative bounds; mistaken distant targets require independent body/shrink evidence rather than an unlimited label fallback.
