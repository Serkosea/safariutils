# Safari Utils v1.3.3

## Fixes

- Added clear client messages when Hypixel API requests fail
- Limited automatic Bazaar price refreshes to once every five minutes

## Downloads

Choose one jar for your Minecraft version:

- `safariutils-1.3.3+mc26.1.2.jar`
- `safariutils-1.3.3-extra+mc26.1.2.jar`
- `safariutils-1.3.3+mc26.2.jar`
- `safariutils-1.3.3-extra+mc26.2.jar`

The regular jars use Safe Mode. Extra includes features in advanced section that provide information the player cannot directly see and may not be safe to use; Minecraft 26.2 builds have received limited testing compared with 26.1.2
