## Additions & Improvements

- Added optional automatic ticket entry when a Sparkling is found before using a ticket, with smooth approach movement and mode-based ticket fallback (`EXTRA ONLY`, off by default).
- Improved coordination between local Sparkling detection and shared-party discoveries to avoid duplicate alerts while preserving ticket reminders.

## Fixes

- Fixed party members' capture effects being mistaken for new Sparklings, leaving duplicate markers and rainbow HUDs.
- Fixed Sparkling beacons remaining above guaranteed catches after the live marker disappears.
- Fixed repeated party-leave attempts when accepting ticket-trading invites.
- Allowed ticket use through party protection whenever an uncaught Sparkling is known before entry.
