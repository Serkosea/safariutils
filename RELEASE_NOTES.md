# Safari Utils v2.2.1

## Changes & Additions

- Added Display Nametags under Display → Waypoints with None, Vanilla, Waypoint, and Both options
- Defaulted the picker to Both so Minecraft and Safari Utils names are visible together
- Applied the choice consistently to live, provisional, Sparkling, tracked, possible-location, and recatch critter markers without changing their hitboxes or tracking

## Downloads

Choose one jar for your Minecraft version:

- `safariutils-2.2.1+mc26.1.2.jar`
- `safariutils-2.2.1-extra+mc26.1.2.jar`
- `safariutils-2.2.1+mc26.2.jar`
- `safariutils-2.2.1-extra+mc26.2.jar`

The regular jars use Safe Mode. Extra includes features in Advanced that may provide information the player cannot directly see and may not be safe to use. Minecraft 26.1.2 builds have received limited testing compared with 26.2.
