# Ticket admission and Normal-edition review

The confirmed SessionManager admission path now invokes TicketEntryNotice, shared by manual and automatic use in all editions. Known local outstanding sparklings or the active party discovery mask qualify. It sends `pc [SU] In Run Now!` for parties, or a local success notice when solo, once per world/visit. Failed clicks and opening a ticket menu do not invoke it. An ordinary admission cannot be retroactively announced when a sparkling appears afterward. Removed the old automatic-only confirmation state and its duplicate responsibilities.

Reviewed the central capability and SafeMode gates, automatic input/focus behavior, candle targeting, local sparkling visibility, marker/outline/glow eligibility, capsule hidden-target prediction, floor-drop classification, Scrappy inference, and mound/source visibility. Normal retains authenticated shared sync but cannot enable Extra controls from imported config. No additional defect was identified in these inspected boundaries. This is static review and regression coverage, not an exhaustive or in-game safety guarantee.

Checks: 26.2 Normal and Extra check; 26.1.2 and 26.3 Normal check; combined 26.2 jar build. Added admission deduplication/ordinary-entry/visit tests and Normal imported-Extra-config restrictions. No deployment.
