## Additions & Improvements

- Added estimated Litterbug return timers and remembered hiding locations.
- Improved texture-based critter detection, Hideyho tracking, and Rockmite mound identification.
- Improved automatic Sparkling ticket entry while unfocused, in menus, or overlapping other players (`EXTRA ONLY`).
- Improved party-sync reconnection and local/chat fallbacks during service outages.

## Fixes

- Fixed Hideyho teleport effects briefly appearing as its final hiding location; valid finds display immediately.
- Fixed duplicate mound markers, incorrect critter sizing, and ordinary markers appearing outside their biome.
- Fixed Sparkling discovery alerts being blocked indefinitely when party sync is unavailable.
- Preserved pre-ticket floor-drop evidence through temporary lobby-information gaps.
- Fixed quest-item visibility for missing or profitable critters and delayed ticket-trading Sparkling announcements.
